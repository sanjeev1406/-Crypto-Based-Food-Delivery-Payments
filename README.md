# Crypto-Based Food Delivery Payments

## Project Description

A decentralized blockchain-based food delivery payment system built on Ethereum that enables transparent, secure, and automated transactions between customers, restaurants, and delivery partners. This smart contract eliminates the need for traditional payment intermediaries by leveraging cryptocurrency for instant settlements.

The platform operates on a trustless model where payments are held in escrow within the smart contract and automatically released to restaurants and delivery partners upon successful order completion. This ensures all parties are protected and compensated fairly.

## Project Vision

To revolutionize the food delivery industry by creating a decentralized, transparent, and efficient payment ecosystem that:

- **Eliminates Payment Delays**: Restaurants and delivery partners receive payments instantly upon order completion
- **Reduces Transaction Costs**: Lower fees compared to traditional payment processors
- **Increases Transparency**: All transactions are recorded on the blockchain, providing complete audit trails
- **Enables Global Reach**: Cryptocurrency payments allow cross-border transactions without currency conversion hassles
- **Empowers Participants**: Gives restaurants and delivery partners more control over their earnings without relying on centralized platforms

Our vision is to create a fairer, more efficient food delivery ecosystem where technology serves all stakeholders equally.

## Key Features

### 1. **Decentralized Order Management**
   - Smart contract handles the entire order lifecycle from placement to delivery
   - Automated status tracking (Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered)
   - Immutable record of all transactions on the blockchain

### 2. **Secure Escrow System**
   - Customer payments are held securely in the smart contract
   - Funds are only released when delivery is confirmed
   - Automatic refund mechanism for cancelled orders

### 3. **Multi-Party Payment Distribution**
   - Automatic splitting of payments between restaurant, delivery partner, and platform
   - Configurable platform fee percentage
   - Real-time balance tracking for all participants

### 4. **Registration & Verification**
   - Platform-managed registration for restaurants and delivery partners
   - Ensures only verified participants can receive orders
   - Prevents fraudulent activities

### 5. **Flexible Withdrawal System**
   - Restaurants and delivery partners can withdraw earnings anytime
   - Gas-efficient batch payment processing
   - Transparent balance management

### 6. **Order Cancellation Protection**
   - Customers, restaurants, or platform can cancel orders
   - Automatic full refund to customers on cancellation
   - Dispute handling capability built into the contract

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Solidity compiler version 0.8.0 or higher
- Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, etc.)
- Development environment (Hardhat, Truffle, or Remix)

### Deployment
1. Compile the `Project.sol` contract
2. Deploy with desired platform fee percentage (e.g., 5 for 5%)
3. Register restaurants and delivery partners using platform owner account
4. Start accepting orders!
